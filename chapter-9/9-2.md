With two jobs, one with 1 ticket and one with 100 tickets, the job with 1 ticket
will often run before the job with 100 tickets completes.

The job with 1 ticket (#0) will have a 1/101 chance of running at each interval.

A ticket imbalance does not allow small jobs with small amount of tickets to run quickly.

There is a `(1 - (1/101 * 100)) * 100` chance. ~99.0099% job #0 will finish before #1
