The -I can cause a jop to hog the CPU. For example `mlfq.py -n 2 -q 100 -a 100 -j 2 -i 1 -S -I -l 0,1000,99:0,1000,0`
with or without -I produces very different results. If `-I` is used,
then Job #0 uses the CPU for 99 out of 100ms until completion and then job #1
runs the rest of the time. If it is not used, Job #0 and #1 switch running every 100ms until they
both complete.
